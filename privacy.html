ShiftRota Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

ShiftRota ("the app") is a shift-scheduling and rotation-planning app. This page explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Data stored on your device

Your shift schedule, rotation patterns, notes, pay settings, and reminder preferences are stored locally on your device. This data is not uploaded to our servers automatically. You may create a backup export (a plain text/JSON file) yourself at any time from Settings, and you are responsible for storing or sharing that file.

Account sign-in (optional)

Creating an account is optional. If you choose to sign in, we use Firebase Authentication (provided by Google) to manage your email and password. We do not see or store your password ourselves.

Referral and promotional features

If you use the Invite & Earn feature, your referral code and referral count are stored in our database (Firebase Firestore) so rewards can be tracked. No schedule data is included.

Notifications and alarms

If you enable shift reminders, the app schedules local notifications or alarms directly on your device using Apple's notification and AlarmKit frameworks. These reminders are generated and delivered entirely on-device; shift content is not sent to any server to generate them.

Calendar sync (optional)

If you enable Calendar Sync, the app writes your shifts to a calendar on your device using Apple's EventKit framework, with your permission. This stays on your device / your iCloud account as applicable to your device settings — we do not receive a copy.

Photo library access (admin only)

Only app administrators can access the photo library, solely to attach an image to an announcement shown to other users. Regular users are never prompted for this permission.

Purchases

Subscriptions are purchased and managed through Apple's App Store (StoreKit). We do not receive your payment details — only entitlement status is confirmed through Apple to unlock Pro features.

What we don't do

  • We do not use third-party advertising networks.
  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.

Data deletion

You can delete your locally stored schedule data at any time from within the app. If you created an account, you can request account deletion by contacting us at the email below.
